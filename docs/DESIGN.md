# 🎨 UI/UX & Design Specification - Trovaya

## 1. Design Philosophy: The 3 Pillars

1. **Access (Frictionless Onboarding):** Sembunyikan jargon Web3 (gas fee, contract address) di balik istilah finansial/bisnis tradisional menggunakan tooltip[cite: 1].
2. **Own & Protect (Clear Safety Indicators):** Tampilkan status proteksi data (misal: *“100% Protected against AI Scraping”*) dengan visual badge hijau yang tegas[cite: 1].
3. **Understand (Transparent AI Auditing):** Semua hasil analisis AI Reviewer wajib disertai badge disclaimer eksplisit bahwa konten bersifat edukatif/audit, bukan saran investasi[cite: 1].

---

## 2. Brand Tokens & Aesthetics

- **Style:** Clean, Trustworthy, Modern Creator Economy (Bukan Crypto-Native Neon).
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: `Space Grotesk` / `Syne`
  - Body & UI: `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Primary (Vault Blue):** `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
  - **Accent (Protection Emerald):** `#10B981` (Emerald 500)
  - **Warning (Unprotected/AI Risk):** `#F59E0B` (Amber 500)
  - **Background:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) / `#020617` (Dark Mode fallback)

---

## 3. Key UI Screen Layouts

### Screen A: Upload & Protect Studio
- **Dropzone:** Drag-and-drop file upload (PNG/JPEG/WEBP).
- **Poison Intensity Slider:** Slider memilih tingkat perturbasi piksel (Low, Medium, High).
- **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Preview gambar asli vs. gambar hasil perturbasi (terbukti 99.9% identik di mata manusia).
- **AI Consent Toggle:** Checkbox *“Izinkan Pelatihan AI dengan Lisensi Berbayar”*[cite: 1].

### Screen B: Marketplace & IP Discovery
- **Card View:** Grid menampilkan thumbnail publik (poisoned), badge status verifikasi ZK-KYC UMKM, dan estimasi biaya lisensi[cite: 1].
- **Detail Page:** Detail hak cipta, simulasi lisensi, tombol *Unlock Original (ZK-Proof)*, dan skor keaslian dari AI Reviewer[cite: 1].

### Screen C: Creator Dashboard
- **IP Portfolio Overview:** Total karya terdaftar, riwayat pendapatan lisensi, dan status badge reputasi[cite: 1].
- **Mock KYC Banner:** Status verifikasi identitas UMKM dengan watermark contoh yang aman[cite: 1].
